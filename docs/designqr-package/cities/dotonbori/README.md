The `references/` directory contains the source manifest, geographic audit, and
coverage diagram linked from the source ledger. It is supporting data, not a
separate documentation area.
The [waterfront reference record](references/waterfront-reference.json) documents
boat dimensions, photo interpretations and representative activity.
The [night reference record](references/night-reference.json) retains the inspected
night photographs, source-envelope frontage fits, ground-floor shop references,
Kani Doraku's first-floor owner photographs and lighting assumptions.
The [Glico animation reference](references/glico-animation-reference.json) records
the inspected track/rainbow photographs, authored timing and frozen QR folding.
The [water motion reference](references/water-motion-reference.json) records
operator wake photographs, boundary limits and the spreading waves' QR integration.
The [cloth motion reference](references/cloth-motion-reference.json) records the
existing interpreted noren/pennant, fixed attachments, authored breeze and QR strips.
The [activity motion reference](references/activity-motion-reference.json) records
the two distinct counters, bridge/boat reactions, tank movement and complete QR poses.
The [Kani sign reference](references/kani-sign-reference.json) records the
street-mounted crab, owner photographs, historical motion footage, full-cycle
clearance and refined restaurant interiors.
The [QR composition record](references/qr-composition.json) records the full-building
visibility contract, complete building inventory and explicitly generated architecture.
The [ground-floor coverage record](references/ground-floor-coverage.json) audits
every source host, the fitted 1F sections and structural exclusions for the saved QR value.
The [façade audit](references/facade-audit.json) records boundary cuts, source-wall
groups, reference margins, fitted riverbank sign panels and full-source extents. The
[façade reference record](references/facade-reference.json) documents inspected
photographs and the current PLATEAU texture-availability check.

135.50125 / latitude 34.66905, triangulation, crop caps, uniform scaling and
diagnostic palette shading. The full research master retains 73 building
objects. The player uses a 144 m square centered on Ebisubashi at local
E4.196907/N2.163932, rotated 45 degrees across the QR tile. The extended source
inventory has 132 buildings in original coordinates. The compact product retains

using O.P. minus 1.3 m, and registers its plan against audited road vertices.
